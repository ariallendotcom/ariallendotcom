01 — The person behind the page

A few threads,
one life.

The résumé version ↗

I keep coming back to the ways we make sense of the world—and of each other.

That thread runs through my work at the Constructive Dialogue Institute, nearly a decade building learning experiences at Amplify, and The Reconstitution Project, a thought experiment in constitutional imagination.

It runs through the writing and the music, too. Different forms. The same curiosity.

A little more background +

My studies span political philosophy at Tufts, public policy at Georgetown Law, and Integral Psychology at the California Institute for Integral Studies. Today, I’m VP of Product at the Constructive Dialogue Institute.
